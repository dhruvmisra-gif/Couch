# Couch

A Netflix-style home for your Stremio library. Browse popular titles, see Continue Watching from your Stremio account, watch trailers, and tap Play to open the title in Stremio.

- Catalogue and artwork: Stremio's official Cinemeta addon
- Library and Continue Watching: your Stremio account (sign in on the Account tab)
- Playback: handled by the Stremio app — Couch doesn't stream anything itself

Install: open the site in Safari (iPhone/iPad) → Share → Add to Home Screen, or in Edge/Chrome on PC → Install app.
